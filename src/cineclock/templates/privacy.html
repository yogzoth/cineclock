Privacy

Short version: cineclock has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It keeps only what it needs to show you showtimes and, if you sign in, your plans and watchlists. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Browsing

You don't need an account to use cineclock. The site doesn't set tracking cookies or run analytics. Posters and backdrops load straight from TMDB, so TMDB sees the same request any website image would. Ticket links take you to each theatre's own site, where its own privacy policy applies.

Letterboxd and IMDb

Connecting either needs an account. To read your public Letterboxd watchlist and diary, your username goes to cineclock's small relay on Cloudflare, which reads those public pages and keeps a copy for up to 6 hours so Letterboxd isn't asked too often. A public IMDb watchlist is read the same way by a scheduled job on GitHub, about once a day or when you press Sync; an uploaded IMDb file stays with your account. Disconnect removes either one.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, cineclock gets your Google account ID and email address from Google, and nothing else from your Google account. It then stores, with your account:

  • the films you plan to go to,
  • your settings, like which rating source you prefer and the profile picture you pick,
  • your Letterboxd username or IMDb watchlist, if you add them,
  • which emails you've turned on, and which films you've already been emailed about.

This lives in a database on Cloudflare. Your browser keeps a sign-in key (a cookie, and a copy in its own storage) so you stay signed in; signing out or deleting your account ends it. cineclock never sees your Google password.

Emails

cineclock only emails you about what you turn on: last chances, your films getting showtimes, a weekly digest or a monthly newsletter. Each is off until you switch it on. Every email has a one-tap unsubscribe link.

AI

Once, when a theatre first lists a film, its public listing text is read by Google's Gemini to spot tags like Q&A or 35mm that the listing words its own way. Only the theatre's listing is sent, never anything about you.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account removes everything listed above, right away. You can also email me and I'll do it for you.
