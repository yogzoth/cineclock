Hi, I'm Jordan.

I built cineclock because I was tired of opening five theatre websites every week to figure out what to see. Now it's one page, updated every few hours.

I'm a self-described gorehound and horror lover with a soft spot for the strange and the sensory. My Letterboxd top four right now: Climax, Belladonna of Sadness, Valerie and Her Week of Wonders and House.

Vancouver's independent and repertory theatres are a big part of why I love living here. If something on this site catches your eye, go see it on the big screen.
